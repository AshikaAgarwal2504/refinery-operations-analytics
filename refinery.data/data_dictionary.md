# Data Dictionary: Refinery Production + Energy (SIMULATED)

> **All data is simulated.** It was generated by `generate_refinery_data.py` from simple rules and random numbers. It is NOT real data from IOCL or any actual refinery. The refinery is fictional (roughly 8 MMTPA-class). Say "simulated data" on your resume, GitHub README and dashboard.

**File:** `refinery_production_energy_simulated.csv`
**Grain:** one row = one process unit on one day
**Size:** 4,386 rows = 6 units x 731 days (2024-01-01 to 2025-12-31), 19 columns, no blank values

## Columns

| # | Column | Type | Meaning | Unit / values |
|---|--------|------|---------|---------------|
| 1 | record_id | integer | Unique row number (primary key) | 1 to 4386 |
| 2 | date | date | Calendar day | YYYY-MM-DD |
| 3 | unit_id | text | Short code of the process unit | CDU, VDU, FCC, HCU, HDT, REF |
| 4 | unit_name | text | Full name of the unit | e.g. Crude Distillation Unit |
| 5 | crude_blend | text | Type of crude being processed that day (plant-wide) | Light Sweet, Medium Sour, Heavy Sour |
| 6 | crude_api_gravity | number | Density measure of the crude; higher = lighter | about 24 to 36 |
| 7 | crude_sulfur_pct | number | Sulfur content of the crude | % by weight |
| 8 | design_capacity_tpd | integer | Maximum the unit is designed to process per day | tonnes/day |
| 9 | feed_processed_tonnes | number | Material actually fed to the unit that day | tonnes |
| 10 | capacity_utilization_pct | number | feed_processed / design_capacity x 100 | % |
| 11 | product_output_tonnes | number | Useful products made from the feed | tonnes |
| 12 | yield_pct | number | product_output / feed x 100 | % |
| 13 | fuel_gas_gj | number | Energy from burning fuel gas in furnaces | GJ (gigajoules) |
| 14 | electricity_mwh | number | Electricity used | MWh |
| 15 | steam_tonnes | number | Process steam used | tonnes |
| 16 | total_energy_gj | number | fuel_gas_gj + electricity_mwh x 3.6 + steam_tonnes x 2.7 | GJ |
| 17 | energy_intensity_gj_per_tonne | number | total_energy_gj / feed_processed. Key efficiency KPI (lower is better) | GJ/tonne |
| 18 | energy_cost_inr | number | Simulated cost of all energy that day | INR |
| 19 | data_source | text | Label that this data is fake | "SIMULATED - not real IOCL data" |

## The six units (what each one does)

| unit_id | Unit | Simple role | Design capacity (tpd) |
|---------|------|-------------|-----------------------|
| CDU | Crude Distillation Unit | First step: heats crude and splits it into fractions (naphtha, kerosene, diesel, residue) | 24,000 |
| VDU | Vacuum Distillation Unit | Splits the heavy residue from the CDU under vacuum | 10,000 |
| FCC | Fluid Catalytic Cracker | Breaks heavy oil into petrol and LPG | 5,000 |
| HCU | Hydrocracker Unit | Cracks heavy oil with hydrogen into diesel and jet fuel | 3,500 |
| HDT | Diesel Hydrotreater | Removes sulfur from diesel | 7,000 |
| REF | Catalytic Reformer | Upgrades naphtha into high-octane reformate | 2,500 |

## Built-in patterns to discover in analysis

1. **Part-load penalty:** energy intensity goes up when utilization drops below about 92%.
2. **Seasonality:** energy intensity is slightly worse in summer (peaks around June and July, lowest around November).
3. **Crude blend effect:** Heavy Sour crude costs more energy and gives lower yield; Light Sweet does the opposite.
4. **Unit differences:** CDU and VDU are the most energy-efficient per tonne; HCU is the most energy-hungry.

## Things to know before analysing

- Do not average `yield_pct` or `energy_intensity_gj_per_tonne` across units directly. Weight by feed: `SUM(product) / SUM(feed)` and `SUM(energy) / SUM(feed)`.
- Conversion factors (1 MWh = 3.6 GJ; steam = 2.7 GJ/tonne) and the price levels are simplified assumptions.
